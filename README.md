# Engineering Exploration

A hands-on exploration of engineering disciplines and the software used to model, simulate, and understand physical systems.

## Areas of exploration

- **Structural mechanics:** Solid mechanics, FEM, stress and deformation.
- **Fluid mechanics:** CFD, flow behaviour, OpenFOAM.
- **Thermal analysis:** Heat transfer and thermal simulation.
- **CAD:** Parametric modelling and engineering design.
- **Robotics:** Control systems, dynamics, and automation.

## Approach

Learn by building small, practical experiments. Compare simulation results with analytical solutions wherever possible, and document assumptions, methods, and findings.

## Current focus

Structural mechanics — validating a cantilever beam simulation in FreeCAD FEM using CalculiX.
