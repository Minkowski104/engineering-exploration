# User and learning context

## Background
- Mechanical engineering degree with a computer-science specialization.
- Several years of professional full-stack software engineering experience.
- Comfortable with programming and software architecture; rebuilding depth and confidence in mechanical engineering fundamentals and engineering simulation.

## Long-term direction
- Explore engineering software and physical systems through practical work.
- Areas of interest include structural mechanics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, CAD, simulation, robotics, and systems related to vehicles and aircraft.
- Interested in work that connects software with engineering analysis and design, rather than remaining exclusively in general-purpose application development.
- Keep the exploration broad initially; do not force an early specialization.

## Learning preferences
- Learn by building and testing small, concrete projects.
- Explain why a step is needed and the physical or numerical meaning behind it.
- Compare simulation outputs with hand calculations or known physical behaviour.
- Prefer step-by-step guidance and focused next actions.
- Be candid about uncertainty; avoid unsupported diagnoses and false precision.
- Balance learning with existing work and other life commitments. Do not assume unlimited study time.

## Hardware preference
- Prefer a Mac-first workflow when it is practical.
- Use Windows or Linux when a tool or workload makes that materially more effective.
